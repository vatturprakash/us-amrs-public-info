Your account & backups

Sign in with Google and explicitly review account backup and restore actions. Different Gmail accounts do not automatically share records.

Why Google access is requested

Google sign-in identifies your account. Optional Google Drive features create and retrieve app backup files using the limited drive.file scope. The app does not request Gmail inbox access or unrestricted access to every Drive file.

See what is stored, how backups work, and your choices.

AMRS assistance with a human review step

The app can prepare report data and assist with supported portal fields. Users complete CAPTCHA and review the report before any official submission. Preparing or previewing a report does not confirm that it has been submitted.
