Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current data handling of US Daily & AMRS Report Update, a Windows and Android application for daily market entries and reports. The maintainer's support contact is prakashagri111@gmail.com.

Information used by the application

  • Google account identity: your verified email address and Google account identifier are used to identify your app account. Google may display your name and profile picture in its own account chooser.
  • Market setup: the district and market name entered when setting up a new account.
  • Records you enter or import: normally crop names, arrivals, prices, calculated values, farmer and consumer counts, dates, and report settings.
  • Optional AMRS login details: a portal mobile number or user name and password, if you choose to save them for portal assistance.

The app does not ask you to give it your Google password. Google sign-in takes place through Google's authorization flow.

Where records are kept

Records and settings are kept in device-local application storage or browser storage. Backups can also be saved to a folder or file you select. When you choose Google Drive Backup or Sync, account records are processed as backup files in your own Google Drive. Local backups may be created automatically after saving a daily entry.

Legacy local Daily Entry records and signed-in account records are separate stores. Switching Gmail does not automatically transfer, delete, or change existing local records. Use the account-specific record and backup controls for the selected account.

Google permissions and their purpose

PermissionPurpose
Basic Google identity, including emailIdentify the signed-in account and keep its account records separate.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.fileCreate or retrieve app backup files and access files explicitly made available to the app.

The application does not request permission to read your Gmail messages or unrestricted access to all Drive files. It does not use Google user data for advertising or sell that data. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Account separation and sharing

A Gmail account is not an authorization to view another Gmail account's records. Matching district or market names do not merge accounts. Backup previews identify new, changed, unchanged, and conflicting records; differing existing records are not silently overwritten by normal Sync.

Data is sent to Google for sign-in and the Drive operations you choose. Data entered on the AMRS portal is processed by that portal's operator. The app does not automatically publish your market records, and it does not send app records to a maintainer-operated central records server.

Credentials and device protection

On Android, saved AMRS credentials use the Android Keystore mechanism and are scoped to the selected account and market. Windows Google OAuth refresh credentials use the operating system credential vault. Optional AMRS credentials on other platforms are stored locally; do not assume every local credential store has the same encryption protection. AMRS passwords and Google OAuth secrets are excluded from normal report-data backups.

Protect access to your device, browser profile, and exported backup files. Testing/debug builds can expose developer tooling and are not represented as hardened public-production releases.

Your choices, retention, and deletion

  • You choose whether to sign in and whether to use Drive backup features.
  • You can sign out of the app and revoke the app's Google authorization in your Google Account connections.
  • You can remove backup files from your own storage and Drive. Revoking Google access does not itself delete existing backup files.
  • Available entry deletion and recovery controls can retain safety copies. Protected records and safety backups may need separate review before removal.
  • Uninstalling the application does not necessarily remove exported files, Windows data folders, or Google Drive backups.

Records and backups remain under your control until removed from their respective storage locations. Contact support if you need help identifying stored records or safety copies.

About these support pages

These pages do not contain sign-in forms, analytics scripts, advertising, or upload forms. If published through GitHub Pages, the hosting provider can process ordinary web-request information under its own privacy terms.

Questions or updates

For privacy questions, contact prakashagri111@gmail.com. Changes to app data handling will be reflected by updating this policy and its date.
