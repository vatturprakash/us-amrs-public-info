Terms of Use

US Daily & AMRS Report Update assists with daily market entries, report preparation, backups, and supported AMRS portal fields. These notes describe how to use the current application responsibly.

Your records and account

Use a Google account and market records that you are entitled to use. Keep your device, account, and backups secure. Choosing a market name does not give you access to another Gmail account's records.

Review before official use

You are responsible for checking dates, crop mapping, arrivals, prices, attendance counts, totals, and report formats against original records before official use. A preview, Excel download, or autofilled field is not confirmation of an official submission.

AMRS portal actions

Portal assistance does not replace your review. Complete CAPTCHA yourself and check the portal's selected date and values. Submit only when you intend to do so and have the required authority. Follow the AMRS operator's own rules and terms. No Google or government endorsement or certification is claimed for this software.

Backups and recovery

Maintain independent backups before changing or importing important data. Review conflicts rather than assuming the newest file is correct. Safety copies may remain after a record changes or is deleted; consider them when managing retention.

Testing and availability

Current builds are undergoing validation. Features may depend on Google authorization, connectivity, device capabilities, and changes to external portals. Public Google OAuth availability is not a statement that every software feature has completed production validation. Contact the maintainer for the status of your build.

Support and privacy
